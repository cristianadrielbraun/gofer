# Brazilian Portuguese glossary

These are the words Gofer's Brazilian Portuguese catalog (`locales/pt-br.json`) uses for its core terms. Keep new messages consistent with them.

The text addresses the reader as *você* ("Escolha", "sua conta"), in the plain, direct register of everyday Brazilian software. Write "e-mail" with a hyphen. Use straight quotes ("), never curly ones. Capitalize only the first word of buttons, titles and headings (sentence case), never Title Case.

## Dates and numbers

Day-month-year order, 24-hour clock, lowercase month and weekday names ("segunda-feira", "janeiro"), decimal comma and dot for thousands.
