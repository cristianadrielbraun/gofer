What we heard

The brief

A local-first email, calendar and contacts client built in Go. Mail stays on your own machine; the app fetches it for you. The mark should feel personal, private and crafted, nod to Go's gopher without becoming a mascot, and keep the copper of the original logo.

privatepersonalcraftedfriendly

Success: reads as mail and as a gopher, owns its copper colour, holds up at 32 px with a dedicated small-size cut, works in one colour.

Final identity

The Eared Seal

A wax seal pressed with an envelope flap and a gopher's ears: your mail, sealed and kept by you.

  • Wax seal = private, local-first mail; the flap keeps it unmistakably about email
  • Two tucked-in ears nod to Go's gopher without drawing a mascot
  • Ear joints and flap edges meet exactly; the stamp is optically centred in the wax
  • Copper carried over from the original logo; one-colour and reversed versions included
  • Dedicated small-size cut with a heavier line for 48 px and below
